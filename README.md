# Amazon Products Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Amazon product sales, revenue drivers, customer reviews, and category performance to support data-driven retail decisions.

## Executive Summary
* **Total YTD Sales:** $2.18M
* **Total QTD Sales:** $811.09K
* **Total Products Sold:** 27.75K Units
* **Total Customer Reviews:** 19.42M

## Key Insights
* **Top Revenue Category:** Men Shoes dominates revenue with **$940.2K (43.18% share)**.
* **Peak Demand:** Sales trend shows a strong upward trajectory during late Q3 through Q4.
* **Top Reviewed Item:** SanDisk 16GB leads in total customer engagement with 0.40M reviews.

## Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Dashboard development, data modeling, dynamic visualizations.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Time intelligence (YTD/QTD), dynamic % contributions.
* **Dataset:** Amazon Sales & Review Records.
